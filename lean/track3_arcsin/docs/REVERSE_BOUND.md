# Square-table erasure and a restricted exact bound

The reference erases nine square-output bits with 21 paid ANDs. The gate set here consists of X, CX, four-T AND, Unand and Unquad, as used by the frozen 512-input square evaluator. Consider circuits that keep 17 particular paid product functions of that eraser and confine every other intermediate function to the linear span of the reference eraser's input, output, and product functions. The four paid products selected for rewriting have zero-based indices 1, 11, 19 and 20. In this family the exact minimum is **21 ANDs**. The original circuit attains the upper bound. Different intermediate functions outside the reference span remain available to unrestricted synthesis.

## Why the four selected products matter

Two are raw input products. The remaining two occur near the end of the reference eraser and use functions involving the square output. The other 17 can be generated without these four. The 9 input bits, 9 square bits and 17 retained products form a 35-dimensional vector space V of Boolean functions on the 512 possible square inputs. All functions are represented by their complete 512-bit truth tables on the normalized square input. Fixing u15 to either value makes the flag a constant; a circuit required to work for both values must also work on this slice. Constant terms in either factor can be dropped modulo the current affine span since (a+c)(b+d) differs from ab by an affine function of a and b.

The quotient of the total allowed space by V has dimension four. A circuit paying for at most three further products can visit a subspace of this quotient with dimension at most three. Here “visit” includes functions later erased or overwritten. There are exactly 66 such subspaces over GF(2), including the zero subspace.

## First obstruction from free erasure

Reverse the circuit from its clean final state and retain every function learned. A reversed linear gate adds only a linear combination. A reversed Unquad adds a function in the quadratic span of the functions already known. A reversed paid AND discards its target and introduces no new function. Consequently the visited space must be generated by repeatedly adjoining its own functions lying in the quadratic span of the current known space, starting from the nine input bits. Each iteration uses exact Gaussian elimination on truth tables.

Only one of the 66 subspaces passes. It is the even-parity hyperplane of the four selected product coordinates, with masks 0, 3, 5, 6, 9, 10, 12 and 15. It remains to determine whether any paid AND can add the first new direction modulo V within that hyperplane.

## Second obstruction from the rank of one AND

A proposed direction s fixes one of seven nonzero classes modulo V. Permit arbitrary linear forms a and b in all 35 live basis functions. The coefficients of their distinct quadratic monomials form an alternating matrix Z, with entries z_ij = a_i b_j + a_j b_i. This matrix has rank at most two, so every four-index Pfaffian vanishes. Its diagonal products are linear functions over GF(2) and enter the permitted correction in V.

For each direction s, a separate exact linear system on the 512 rows describes **all** alternating coefficient vectors that evaluate to the chosen direction up to an arbitrary linear correction. Its 595 entries are affine functions of 190 binary parameters. Substitution turns every four-index Pfaffian into a Boolean polynomial of degree at most two. Gaussian elimination finds a set whose XOR equals the constant polynomial one. The simultaneous vanishing of all Pfaffians would give 0 = 1. This excludes every possible first AND, even if all 17 retained paid products are already resident and an arbitrary affine output correction is free.

The proof generator is pfaffian.py. The seven XOR witnesses in pfaffian_witness.json list four-index subsets by zero-based lexicographic index. The separate checker verify_pfaffians.py reconstructs the truth tables from the reference eraser, enumerates the 66 subspaces and tests each polynomial identity on all 18,146 binary parameter assignments of Hamming weight zero, one or two. These assignments determine a Boolean polynomial of degree at most two. The checker uses neither the producer's polynomial expansion nor its elimination procedure for the Pfaffian identities. Run python -B verify_pfaffians.py from this directory to regenerate reverse_obstruction_receipt.json.

## Earlier bounded searches

The inexpensive precursor checked all 38 erasure-admissible hyperplanes among 127 hyperplanes retaining the reference gate controls. At each stalled synthesis space it tested every nonzero linear combination of the original eighteen input/output functions as left factor and an unrestricted right factor from the current space. None added a direction. A second sweep on the 17-product case allowed up to two of the paid functions in the left factor as well. Its 40,370,175 left factors also yielded no bridge. The Pfaffian certificate removes that factor-support restriction for the stated four-product rewrite.
