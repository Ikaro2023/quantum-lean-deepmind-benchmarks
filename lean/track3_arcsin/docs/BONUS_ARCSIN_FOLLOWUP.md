# Arcsin K25 follow-up

The screened constructions yielded no replacement for the submitted 366,200 bound. The baseline has 3,662 T gates on 100 qubits. Its published compiler and checker receipts remain in the read-only Track 3 project.

| Search | Fresh result |
| --- | --- |
| Narrow arithmetic, width of high part 4 | Replayed the carry-count screen. Its model prices both widths 4 and 8 at 236 ANDs. The physical width-8 path uses 98 + 50 + 90 = 238 ANDs. The symbolic bag includes a separately computed low-bit helper in the first column. The screen does not generate gates, implement the extra helper or certify the high-part output/uncompute and scratch schedule. |
| Fixed symbolic arithmetic traces | All 98 forward, 50 middle and 90 reverse ANDs in the narrow path, plus all 166 ANDs in the late-addition stage, produce symbols outside the current affine wire span. None can be replaced directly by affine operations on the existing symbolic trace. Every one of the 21 candidate erase/recompute intervals in the middle stage crosses a full-rank state where the old product is independent. |
| After the stage-three adder | Replayed 1,926 operations across all 440 packed coefficient bins. The outgoing tableau and zero phase match exactly. All 16 paid ANDs are affine-independent of the available wires immediately before use. The structured-selector search reproduced 23,395 partitions with at most 32 groups. The best 28-group partition still needs 20 missing indicator dimensions. |
| Late arithmetic stage | Replayed the previous burst-count DP. The nominal 154-AND row leaves 22 input bits unabsorbed. A fresh mixed integer screen enforced absorption of every column and the 39-slot budget. It tested each of the 18 placements of the eight special helper products in three through six zero-return bursts. Every model reported infeasibility below 166 ANDs. The solver is allowed to delay use of helper output bits and to make them available before the low input has been finalized. With 200 scratch slots, its single-burst positive control completed and its checked integer witness costs 133 ANDs. These solver statuses concern the stated carry recurrence and finite search domain. |
| Fewer than 100 qubits | The submitted 449-case and 4,705-case traces both reach 50 nonzero ancilla columns at eight stages: s3a, s3bAdd, s3bPost, s4_2, s4_3, s5_1, s5_2Core and s5_3. The replayed local peeling order frees wire 86. After it, none of wires 80–85 belongs to the remaining quadratic span. This single-stage observation leaves other simultaneous peaks and the cost of recomputing the released wire. |

The local searches cover specific synthesis families and sampled liveness. General circuit minimality and a universal qubit lower bound remain open. The bonus produced Python and solver receipts. It produced no new Lean theorem or lower public cost.

Run `python -B screen.py` and `python -B burst_milp.py` in this directory. The first command runs the original analysis programs as isolated read-only inputs and records their hashes and output digests in `results.json`. It also performs the fixed-trace algebra checks. The second program records the checked positive witness in `burst_control.json` and each solver result in `burst_milp.json`.
